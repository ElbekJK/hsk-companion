HSK Companion is an unofficial, offline-first Chinese language study app. The current v1.0.0 development build does not create user accounts, display advertisements, include third-party analytics SDKs, or request Android Internet permission. Vocabulary progress, completed learning sessions, quiz answers and on-device study statistics are stored locally in Android application preferences. Users may export progress to a portable, unencrypted JSON file through Android’s document picker and may restore it with explicit confirmation. Backups are stored at locations chosen by users and should be kept private. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes locally held learning progress, unless a separate backup was saved.

To pronounce Mandarin words or example sentences, HSK Companion sends the text to the user's selected Android text-to-speech engine. The engine is provided by the device or a third party and may use network services depending on its settings; that processing is governed by the TTS engine provider's privacy terms, not solely by this app.

Current v1.0.0 does not transmit study progress to a developer-operated server and does not provide cloud sync. This draft must be revised before publishing if permissions, crash reporting, analytics, accounts, subscriptions or external services are added.

This is a draft only: final Google Play publication requires a publicly available privacy-policy URL and an accurate, matching Data safety declaration.
